Only the original collector's `Diagnostics.prepare_emission` may issue the capability. Its original collector ledger retains the complete already-authenticated planner result, first/final capture truth, final StateDiagnosis, immutable EmitterRequest and exact original MemoryDocumentTarget. Trusted public owner configuration/validation calls occur outside collector IO; no peer private field/validator import is permitted. Read-only accessor repeats before consumption allocate no resources or grants. The accessor grants no target effect, capture ACK or publication authority. [YWE-REQ-0049]

Single-use claim remains in the original collector ledger inside existing effectful `module_capture` admission; there is no additional public claim port. Whole raw/resource/token preflight precedes claim and effects. Actual uncertain admission with a genuine issued scope retains its consumed association and once-only cleanup; a refusal with no issued scope cannot manufacture a claim, ACK or target action. Existing exact Core child plus IssuedEmitterCaptureControl validation remains required, with both provided dispositions ACTIVE around ordinary capture. [YWE-REQ-0049]
