# Wolf Companion Scene Plan

Status: `phase_16_recovery`

## Canon requirement

The wolves physically walk with the player. They appear in visions, assist in quests, fight at the player's side, signal major changes, cannot truly die, and may temporarily decohere before returning.

## Ravenfall scenes

```text
Approach scene:
  Both wolves react to the threshold pressure before the player understands it.

Discovery scene:
  White Wolf reveals oath-pattern light while Dark Wolf anchors the player against Shadow pressure.

Reveal path:
  White Wolf assists by exposing contradictions and protecting the player from backlash.

Conceal path:
  Dark Wolf assists by masking the oath from hostile witnesses and holding memory under pressure.

Bind path:
  Both wolves coordinate; neither dominates.

Combat scene:
  Wolves assist physically against threshold-touched enemies or oath-warped attackers.

Decoherence scene:
  If pushed too far, a wolf may temporarily lose coherence, fade, and later return. This is not death.
```
