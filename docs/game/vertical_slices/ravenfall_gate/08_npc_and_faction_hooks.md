# Ravenfall Gate NPC and Faction Hooks

Status: `phase_16_recovery`

## NPC candidate roles

```text
Public Witness
  Appears when the oath is revealed.

Hidden Keeper
  Appears when the oath is concealed or studied.

Threshold Guard
  Reacts to bind/threshold stabilization outcomes.

Oath Debtor
  Has relation to the oath and may trigger A1/A4 pressure.

Faction Agent
  Attempts to weaponize or suppress the oath.
```

## Faction reactions

Factions may form claims, beliefs, or leverage around the oath. Faction claims are not automatically truth.

## Rule

Meaningful NPCs must have relation context. Conscious NPCs need self-reference classification.
