# Realm Truth Boundary Contract

## Purpose

This contract defines the non-negotiable separation between **canonical world truth**, **realm law**, **perception**, **myth**, **prophecy**, **faction interpretation**, and **authored override controls** in the Yggdrasil World Engine. Its purpose is to prevent downstream systems, tools, and content authors from collapsing distinct truth layers into a single mutable narrative surface.

This file is authoritative for boundary behavior across:
- realm mechanics
- perception overlays
- myth emergence
- prophecy activation
- faction topology
- NPC synthesis
- quest generation
- authored overrides and tooling

## Core Principle

YWE is not a system where reality is rewritten casually. Meaningful variation emerges through lawful interpretation, manifestation, resonance, memory, and access — not by erasing canonical truth.

## Authority Order

For repository structure and design-baseline questions, consult
`docs/master_specification/YWE_MASTER_SPECIFICATION.md` together with
`docs/project/repository_map.md` before applying the layer precedence below.

When two layers appear to conflict, precedence resolves in this order:

1. **Canonical Cosmology and Fixed World Truth**
2. **Realm Law and Realm Mechanics**
3. **Persistent Worldstate Delta**
4. **ASH-Derived Pattern Interpretation**
5. **Faction Topology and Collective Positioning**
6. **Myth Emergence**
7. **Prophecy Activation**
8. **Perception Overlay**
9. **Authored Overrides**
10. **Presentation / UI / Tooling Views**

Lower layers may reveal, filter, frame, weight, obscure, or interpret higher layers, but may not invalidate them.

## Truth Layers

### 1. Canonical World Truth
Canonical world truth consists of facts that are true whether or not any actor understands them.

Includes:
- cosmological structure
- realm existence and lawful relation
- canonical geography
- persistent historical events once canonized
- fixed lineage truths once resolved
- stable site identity at the canonical level

Canonical world truth is not altered by rumor, vision, ideology, faction doctrine, or temporary overlay.

### 2. Realm Truth
Realm truth defines what each realm is, what laws govern it, how boundaries behave, and what forms of manifestation are possible within or across it.

Realm truth may:
- constrain what can manifest
- alter what can be perceived under lawful conditions
- allow boundary thinning, resonance, or bleed under defined conditions

Realm truth may not:
- erase canonical world truth
- reclassify a falsehood into truth
- convert subjective experience into objective history without canonization rules

### 3. Persistent Worldstate Delta
Persistent worldstate delta records lawful consequences of actions, revelations, rituals, defeats, reforms, successions, discoveries, bindings, and site changes.

Worldstate delta may:
- change who holds power
- change which sites are active, suppressed, threatened, or consecrated
- change what pressures are present in a region or faction
- unlock or suppress future possibility

Worldstate delta may not:
- retroactively falsify canon
- rewrite cosmology
- override realm law

### 4. Myth Layer
Myth is a meaning-bearing reinterpretive layer that changes how events are remembered, framed, inherited, or retold.

Myth may:
- elevate certain events into symbolic significance
- change public memory and ritual weight
- attach archetypal meaning to places, persons, and acts

Myth may not:
- directly rewrite what factually occurred
- replace worldstate delta
- serve as sole proof that a false event became true

### 5. Prophecy Layer
Prophecy is a future-weighting layer. It influences expectation, pressure, readiness, fear, ritual preparation, and interpretive momentum.

Prophecy may:
- bias actor behavior
- increase manifestation likelihood under lawful conditions
- alter preparedness and symbolic alignment

Prophecy may not:
- guarantee occurrence regardless of law
- replace causality
- retroactively define past truth

### 6. Faction Claim Layer
Factions make claims about truth, legitimacy, interpretation, destiny, ownership, and memory.

Faction claims may:
- affect recruitment, conflict, suppression, doctrine, ritual enforcement, and public narrative
- alter access, pressure, and visibility

Faction claims may not:
- become truth merely by dominance
- overwrite canonical geography or realm law
- nullify contradiction

### 7. Perception Overlay Layer
Perception overlays define what a player, NPC, faction cohort, bloodline, oathbound lineage, attuned actor, or conditioned observer experiences or can interpret.

Perception overlays may:
- reveal hidden meaning
- conceal true significance
- reskin symbolic meaning for a subjectively valid experience
- expose lawful divergences in a multiplayer-safe way

Perception overlays may not:
- rewrite shared canonical geography
- create shared historical facts ex nihilo
- convert subjective hallucination into objective state without supporting canon pathways

### 8. Authored Override Layer
Authored overrides are constrained production controls for pacing, curation, emphasis, testability, and designed narrative intention.

Authored overrides may:
- pin presentation priorities
- bias selection among valid lawful options
- protect required authored sequences
- force exposure order where lawful options already exist

Authored overrides may not:
- break cosmology
- invalidate realm law
- force impossible manifestation
- convert non-canonical claims into fact
- bypass worldstate persistence without explicit canonical authority

## Boundary Rules

### Rule A: Truth Is Not Perception
A thing may be truly present and not widely perceived.
A thing may be vividly perceived and not canonically true.
Systems must preserve this distinction.

### Rule B: Myth Is Not History
Myth can reshape significance and inheritance of memory, but not the factual substrate of what occurred.

### Rule C: Prophecy Is Not Guarantee
Prophecy creates directional pressure, not unconditional inevitability.

### Rule D: Claims Are Not Facts
Faction doctrine, rumor, archive control, and political legitimacy do not automatically define canon.

### Rule E: Realm Conditions Are Not Arbitrary Magic
Any realm shift, thinning, bleed, or threshold transition must satisfy realm-mechanics conditions.

### Rule F: Overrides Are Not Authorial Omnipotence
Author tools may select within law. They may not replace law.

## Cross-System Contracts

### Realm Mechanics ↔ Perception
Realm mechanics determines what is lawfully available for manifestation.
Perception determines what an observer can register, decode, survive, or misread.

A perception system may only surface states permitted by realm law.

### Realm Mechanics ↔ Myth
Realm states may intensify the symbolic weight of an event or site, but myth remains an interpretive and memorial layer.

### Realm Mechanics ↔ Prophecy
Realm conditions may increase or suppress the possibility that prophetic pressure manifests, but prophecy never overrides impossible conditions.

### Faction Topology ↔ Truth
Faction topology tracks power, doctrine, legitimacy, claims, and pressure. It does not define ontology.

### Authored Overrides ↔ Everything
Overrides must declare:
- target scope
- duration
- reason
- authority source
- affected systems
- explicit non-overrides

Any override lacking those declarations is invalid.

## Multiplayer Safety Rules

Different players may experience different overlays, emphasis, omens, symbolic readings, or hidden presences.

However, shared simulation must retain consistency for:
- canonical geography
- shared site state
- persistent consequences
- faction structural state
- resolved historical outcomes
- realm-lawful transition events once committed to worldstate

Subjective divergence is allowed.
Shared truth divergence is not, unless mediated through a canonical branch or explicitly designed shard rule outside the current YWE base contract.

## Canonization Thresholds

A lower layer may influence canon only through a lawful canonization path.

Required canonization path elements:
- source event or condition
- validation trigger
- persistence rule
- cross-system reconciliation
- contradiction resolution
- worldstate commit

Without these, myths remain myths, prophecies remain pressures, claims remain claims, and perceptions remain perceptions.

## Contradiction Handling

When systems disagree:

1. Check canonical truth first.
2. Check realm law second.
3. Check committed worldstate delta third.
4. Identify whether the contradiction belongs to myth, prophecy, faction claim, or perception.
5. Preserve the contradiction if it is diegetically valid.
6. Reject the contradiction if a lower layer attempts to overwrite a higher one.

Not every contradiction is a bug. Some contradictions are intended and meaningful, but they must remain layer-correct.

## Validation Requirements

All future systems touching truth surfaces must validate:
- separation of truth and perception
- separation of myth and history
- separation of prophecy and causality
- separation of faction claims and ontology
- authored override scope legality
- realm-law legality for transitions and manifestations
- worldstate persistence integrity

## Forbidden Design Moves

The following are hard failures:
- turning perception overlays into world rewrites
- letting faction control define reality by assertion alone
- using prophecy as guaranteed script completion
- using myth as retroactive fact replacement
- allowing authored content to bypass canonical law without declared authority
- treating realm behavior as cosmetic flavor text rather than lawful constraint
- collapsing all truth surfaces into a single lore layer

## Implementation Guidance

When a downstream file or tool is ambiguous, it must default upward:
- ask what is canonically true
- ask what realm law permits
- ask what worldstate has committed
- only then apply interpretation, weighting, and presentation

This contract exists to keep YWE coherent under growth. It is meant to be restrictive.
