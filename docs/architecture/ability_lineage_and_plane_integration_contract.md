# Ability Lineage and Plane Integration Contract

Abilities may emerge from plane attunement and lineage resonance.

## Plane-linked abilities

Plane-linked abilities should reflect simulation-layer categories:

```text
Physical: embodiment, survival, material interaction
Etheric: vitality, field sensing, healing presence
Astral: dreams, symbols, visions, emotional imagery
Mental: thought, self-model, pattern interpretation
Causal: consequence, oaths, branch traces, fate threads
Celestial: coherence, harmony, completion, revelation
Shadow: concealment, hidden truth, integration, containment
Void: entropy pressure, reset, dissolution boundary
Divine Core: blueprint, origin, high-order pattern law
```

## Lineage-linked abilities

Lineage resonance opens eligibility and interpretation. It does not determine destiny.
