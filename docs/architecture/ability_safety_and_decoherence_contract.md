# Ability Safety and Decoherence Contract

Ability systems must support risk without destructive failure.

## Decoherence

Decoherence is temporary loss of coherence in an ability, companion manifestation, branch access, or source relation.

## Wolf rule

Wolves cannot be killed. Ability overstrain may temporarily reduce wolf coherence or manifestation, but recovery must be possible.

## Recovery support

ASH Pattern System diagnostics, recovery, containment, and safe fallback may support ability stabilization.

## Forbidden

```text
permanent deletion of a wolf
ability cost: kill wolf
soft-locking ability progression
unrecoverable coherence collapse without containment or fallback
```
