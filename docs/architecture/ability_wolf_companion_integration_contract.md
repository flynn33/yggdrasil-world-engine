# Ability and Twin Wolf Companion Integration Contract

The Ability / Power Engine must preserve wolf companion canon.

## Correct model

The White Wolf and Dark Wolf physically accompany the player. They may assist in quest completion and combat. They cannot be killed. They may temporarily decohere and later return.

## Ability integration forms

```text
white_wolf_revelation_assist
white_wolf_protection_assist
white_wolf_pattern_visibility

dark_wolf_anchor_assist
dark_wolf_containment_assist
dark_wolf_memory_assist

twin_wolf_threshold_bridge
twin_wolf_coherence_restoration
twin_wolf_branch_stabilization
```

## Forbidden

```text
white_wolf_good_power
dark_wolf_evil_power
wolf_morality_meter
wolf_death_cost
wolf_enemy_conversion
single_wolf_lockout_as_default
```
