# Twin Wolf Companion Canon Contract

Date: 2026-05-19
Status: canonical source-truth contract

## Core statement

The White Wolf and Dark Wolf are complementary opposites, but not moral opposites.

They are not good and evil.
They are not a morality system.
The player may be good or evil; the wolves are not moral poles.

## Companion embodiment

The wolves physically walk with the player where canonically appropriate.

They may:

- appear beside the player;
- assist quest completion;
- assist in combat;
- appear in visions;
- warn or guide;
- signal major changes;
- respond to branch and consequence states;
- temporarily withdraw if decohered.

## Phase 15A manifestation boundary

Phase 15A distinguishes wolf resonance from wolf presence. White Wolf and Dark
Wolf may physically accompany the player when canonically appropriate, but
runtime presence is expressed through conditional manifestations with explicit
trigger, duration, dismissal, and source-reference evidence.

They are not default party members. Their appearance must be justified by
quest, branch, location threshold, wolf resonance, myth, prophecy, combat, or
ability pressure rather than assumed as permanent baseline presence.

## Functional poles

White Wolf:

- illuminates;
- protects;
- reveals;
- clarifies;
- exposes hidden patterns.

Dark Wolf:

- endures;
- conceals;
- transforms;
- contains;
- carries depth, gravity, and memory.

## Mutual need

Each wolf carries what the other needs.

Neither wolf is complete alone. Neither is the correct or moral choice. The wisest path is balance and integration.

## Death and decoherence

The wolves cannot be killed.

When overwhelmed or defeated in manifestation, a wolf may lose coherence temporarily.

Correct state:

```text
temporary decoherence
```

Not:

```text
permanent death
```

After decoherence, the wolf can recover and return.

## Forbidden

- Rejected: White Wolf = good;
- Rejected: Dark Wolf = evil;
- Rejected: wolf morality meter;
- Rejected: corruption / purity meter;
- Rejected: permanent wolf death;
- Rejected: one wolf becomes the enemy;
- Rejected: choose one wolf forever;
- Rejected: wolves are only abstract variables;
- Rejected: wolves are not opposites;
- Rejected: wolves cannot assist in combat or quests.
