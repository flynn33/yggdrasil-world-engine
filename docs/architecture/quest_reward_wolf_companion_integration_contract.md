# Quest Reward Wolf Companion Integration Contract

Quest rewards may affect the wolves as embodied companions.

## Canon

The White Wolf and Dark Wolf are complementary opposites, not good and evil.
They walk with the player, assist in quests and combat, appear in visions, and
cannot truly die. They may temporarily decohere and later return.

## Reward effects

```text
white_wolf_presence_update
dark_wolf_presence_update
twin_wolf_coherence_update
quest_assist_availability
combat_assist_readiness
vision_signal
manifestation_rule_update
decoherence_pressure
recovery_pressure
companion_memory_update
```

## Forbidden

```text
Reject: morality-score framing for either wolf
Reject: reward-color alignment as moral classification
Reject: permanent loss of either wolf as reward cost
Reject: choosing one wolf forever
```
