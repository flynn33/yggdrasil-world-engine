# Wolf Companion Playtest Validation Contract

Phase 17 validates that wolves are present as embodied companions in Ravenfall Gate traces.

## Canon requirements

- Both wolves may be physically present.
- They assist the player in quest completion and combat/threat contexts.
- They can signal through visions.
- They cannot truly die.
- They may temporarily decohere and return.
- They are not good and evil.
- They are not a morality system.

## Trace requirement

Every path must include a `WolfCompanionTrace` or explicitly explain why a wolf is in a vision/perception-only state for that moment.
