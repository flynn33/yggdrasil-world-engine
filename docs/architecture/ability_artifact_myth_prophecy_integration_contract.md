# Ability Artifact, Myth, and Prophecy Integration Contract

Abilities may be shaped by artifact bindings, myth participation, and prophecy exposure.

## Artifact influence

Artifact bindings may channel, stabilize, transform, seal, or risk an ability.

## Myth influence

Myth participation may increase ability visibility, cultural interpretation, or branch pressure.

## Prophecy influence

Prophecy exposure may create ability pressure or activation windows, but prophecy must not become a guaranteed script.
