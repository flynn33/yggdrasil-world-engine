## Cosmology Summary

Before the Divine Core there was only primordial darkness.
Consciousness gathered within that darkness until dark matter compacted into the
first conscious singularity, the **Dark Star**.

The Dark Star created gravity.
Increasing gravity created time.
Void formed around the Dark Star as containment.
The Dark Star then collapsed into the first light, the **Divine Core**.

This event created the nine fixed realms or planes:

1. Divine Core
2. Celestial
3. Causal
4. Mental
5. Astral
6. Etheric
7. Physical
8. Shadow
9. Void

In YWE canon, **realm** and **plane** are equivalent terms.
The realms are layered states of existence separated by liminal space and
visualized symbolically through ASH cosmology and Yggdrasil.

## Canonical Rules

- Realms are fixed cosmological states and do not change structure
- Players do not move the realms; players change resonance with realms
- The Divine Core is both the origin center of reality and one of the nine realms or planes
- The first wolves emerge with Divine Core creation to preserve balance against decoherence and Void
