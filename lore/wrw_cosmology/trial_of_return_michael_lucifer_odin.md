# Trial of Return, Michael, Lucifer, and Odin

Date: 2026-03-14
Project: Yggdrasil World Engine
Status: canonical lore authority

## Purpose

Defines the locked canon for the First War, the Great Trial, Michael and
Lucifer after return, Odin's mortal-origin ritual path, Yggdrasil, and the
mortal-instancing model for celestial beinghood.

## First War and Its Consequence

The First War did not end with the Grand Architect surviving in isolation inside
the Divine Core.

The locked canon is:

- the Grand Architect is not corrupt
- he is an endgame mystery
- he did not survive the First War
- he ended it by sacrifice

Public-facing and early-stage lore should preserve mystery around the full truth
of that sacrifice rather than revealing every consequence immediately.

## Celestial Exile and the Great Trial

Michael and Lucifer became major celestial leaders after the First War.

Their philosophical differences led to the Second War.

Celestials then fell into mortal incarnation and forgetting through the
**Great Trial**.
Through mortal suffering, death, rebirth, and return, Michael and Lucifer were
changed by guilt, grief, pain, responsibility, and consequence.

## Michael and Lucifer After Return

Michael and Lucifer ultimately reconciled after the Great Trial.

Lucifer is not a lord of evil and not an evil sovereign.
He becomes keeper of the **Seventh Gate**, a guide of the fallen, a light-bringer
in darkness, and a guardian against final loss into Void.

## Odin's Mortal-Origin Ritual Path

Odin is unique.
He began as a mortal.

Through ritual means rooted in mortal life, he learned to reach liminal space by
way of the **Mental Realm**.
These means included roots, berries, mushrooms, and related ritual practice.

In liminal space, Odin encountered a **Dark Wolf**.
Dark Wolf taught him a rite for unlocking great power from within himself.

That rite required:

- sacrifice of an eye
- hanging himself from a tree for nine days

During the rite, Dark Wolf defended Odin's celestial soul from reapers.
Without that intervention, reapers would have collected the departing celestial
soul for reincarnation or dragged it toward Shadow according to its heaviness.

Michael then met Odin in liminal space and granted him:

- great power
- the runes
- knowledge for creating others like himself

Odin became All-Father of **his** pantheon.
He is not the source of all pantheons, and not all pantheons were made by Odin.

## Yggdrasil and Liminal Space

**Yggdrasil** is the correct spelling.

Yggdrasil is both symbolic and real connective architecture through which the
realms are understood and crossed.
Its structure is central to threshold travel, liminal passage, and the ordering
of layered existence.

The wolves tend its roots because they preserve balance across that layered
structure.

## Mortal Instancing and Celestial Return

A mortal is an instantiated expression of a celestial being.

A celestial may instantiate as one mortal life or many.
Alternate decision branches may produce alternate instances.
While mortal, those instances usually do not know of each other.

When they return, their knowledge and experience reintegrate into the greater
celestial identity.

Non-mortal reality is nonlinear.
Time outside the mortal realm is not linear.

## Locked Canon Rules

- Michael and Lucifer are not reducible to simple good-versus-evil archetypes.
- Lucifer's Seventh Gate role must remain canonically intact.
- Odin's path is mortal-origin ritual ascent, not belief-only spontaneous creation.
- Yggdrasil is connective architecture, not decorative symbolism alone.
- Mortal instancing and reintegration must remain compatible with the player-as-mortal starting model.
