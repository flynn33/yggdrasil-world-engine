# First Darkness and Divine Core

Date: 2026-03-14
Project: Yggdrasil World Engine
Status: canonical lore authority

## Purpose

Defines the locked origin cosmology for primordial darkness, the Dark Star, the
Divine Core, the nine realms or planes, the first Architects, and the emergence
of the first wolves.

## Primordial Darkness

Before the Divine Core there was only darkness.

Within that darkness, consciousness began to gather.
Dark matter compacted around that gathered consciousness until the first
conscious singularity formed:

- the **Dark Star**

## Dark Star, Gravity, Time, and Void

The formation of the Dark Star created gravity.

As gravity intensified, time emerged.

Void formed around the Dark Star as containment.
Void is therefore not prior order or rival sovereignty.
It is the enclosing condition around the first conscious singularity.

## Collapse into the Divine Core

The Dark Star collapsed and burst into the first light:

- the **Divine Core**

This event created the nine fixed realms or planes.
In YWE canon, **realm** and **plane** are equivalent terms.
`Realm` remains the preferred repository term.

## The Nine Realms or Planes

The nine realms are:

1. Divine Core
2. Celestial
3. Causal
4. Mental
5. Astral
6. Etheric
7. Physical
8. Shadow
9. Void

These realms are:

- layered states of existence
- separated by liminal space
- formless in themselves
- visualized symbolically through ASH cosmology and Yggdrasil
- fixed and unchanging in structure

Realms do not act, evolve, or rewrite themselves.
Change happens in beings, resonance, perception, access, and consequence.

## Architects and the First Wolves

The consciousnesses contained within the Dark Star were transformed into the
first **Architects**.

The first White Wolves and Dark Wolves emerged from the union of:

- light
- dark
- consciousness
- primordial matter
- primordial energy

They emerged to preserve balance against decoherence and Void across the layered
structure of existence.

## Grand Architect Staging Rule

The Grand Architect is not corrupt and did not remain alive in isolation inside
the Divine Core after the First War.

The locked canon is that he ended the First War by sacrifice.
Public-facing and early-stage lore should preserve that event as mystery rather
than exposing the later distributed-fragment reveal too early.

## Locked Canon Rules

- The Divine Core is both the origin center of reality and one of the nine realms or planes.
- Realms and planes are not separate ontology categories in YWE canon.
- The realms are fixed layered states and do not change structure.
- White Wolf and Dark Wolf do not predate primordial darkness as separate rival sovereigns.
- The first wolves emerge with Divine Core creation from the union named above.
