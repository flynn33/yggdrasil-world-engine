## Provenance Rules

1. Canonical assertions must cite one of the two canonical lore authorities by
   repository path and relevant heading.
2. `canon_scope.md` determines whether an assertion belongs to WRW, YWE Core,
   or the upstream ASH dependency; it does not replace the cited lore text.
3. `README.md`, the master specification, glossary entries, examples, and game
   documents are derivative surfaces unless they are separately declared as
   controlling authorities for their own scope.
4. A derivative surface may summarize or route canonical lore but may not add
   an unrecorded event, motive, identity, chronology, or cosmological rule.
5. A game-facing reveal may intentionally withhold the distributed-fragment
   consequences of the Grand Architect's sacrifice. Withholding a reveal does
   not change the canonical event.
6. Spelling and identity normalization must preserve `Yggdrasil`, Lucifer's
   Seventh Gate role, Odin's limited pantheon scope, and the equivalence of
   `realm` and `plane`.

## Admission Rule for New Sources

A new source may enter this register only when it has an explicit authority
role, repository path, subject boundary, provenance, and conflict resolution
against the existing canonical sources. Notes, generated prose, runtime output,
and external summaries remain non-authoritative until that review occurs.
