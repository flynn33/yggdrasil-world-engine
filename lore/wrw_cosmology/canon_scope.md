# WRW Cosmology Canon Scope

Date: 2026-07-19
Project: Yggdrasil World Engine
Status: canonical WRW reference-profile boundary

## Purpose

This record defines what the Where Ravens Wait: Eternal Reckoning cosmology
profile may assert as locked story canon and separates that story canon from
YWE Core contracts and the upstream ASH dependency.

## Canonical Sources

The locked WRW cosmology in this directory is defined by:

- `first_darkness_and_divine_core.md` for primordial darkness, the Dark Star,
  the Divine Core, the nine realms or planes, the first Architects, and the
  emergence of the first wolves;
- `trial_of_return_michael_lucifer_odin.md` for the First War, the Great Trial,
  Michael, Lucifer, Odin, Yggdrasil, mortal instancing, and celestial return.

`README.md` is an informative directory summary. It routes readers to the two
canonical sources but cannot add or override canon.

## In Scope

This WRW reference profile owns the named creation narrative, characters,
relationships, mysteries, ritual history, and game-facing interpretations in
the canonical source files. Its locked boundaries include:

- primordial darkness precedes the Dark Star and the first light;
- the Dark Star is the first conscious singularity, whose formation creates
  gravity and whose intensification gives rise to time;
- Void forms as containment around the Dark Star;
- the Dark Star collapses into the Divine Core and the nine fixed realms or
  planes are created;
- `realm` and `plane` name one ontology category, with `realm` preferred in
  repository prose;
- the first Architects are transformed consciousnesses from the Dark Star;
- White Wolves and Dark Wolves emerge with Divine Core creation from light,
  dark, consciousness, primordial matter, and primordial energy;
- the Grand Architect ends the First War by sacrifice and is not corrupt;
- Michael and Lucifer reconcile after the Great Trial, and Lucifer retains the
  Seventh Gate role;
- Odin follows the recorded mortal-origin ritual path and becomes All-Father
  of his pantheon, not the source of every pantheon;
- Yggdrasil is real and symbolic connective architecture through liminal
  space; and
- mortal instances reintegrate into their greater celestial identity after
  return, while non-mortal reality is nonlinear.

## Out of Scope

This profile does not own or redefine:

- ASH mathematics, axioms, state-space semantics, codewords, diagnostics, or
  generation-planning behavior;
- YWE Core interfaces, platform-neutral state contracts, conformance rules, or
  host-adapter boundaries;
- platform implementation, rendering, input, storage, networking, or product
  build behavior;
- unrecorded motives, chronology, identities, pantheons, or cosmological
  mechanisms not established by a canonical source; or
- presentation reveals whose timing is intentionally preserved as mystery.

## Cross-Scope Rules

The nine-coordinate base ontology MUST remain immutable. [YWE-REQ-0005]
Perception state MUST NOT rewrite shared truth. [YWE-REQ-0007]
A coordinate index or bit position, an ordinal, a presentation order, a realm
or plane identity, and a full vector or state identity MUST be treated as
distinct concepts and MUST NOT be inferred from one another without an
explicit mapping. [YWE-REQ-0008]
A WRW-specific rule MUST NOT become normative YWE Core truth. [YWE-REQ-0015]

1. WRW content may instantiate or interpret a YWE Core capability but cannot
   turn a game-specific name, character, plot, or destination into a universal
   Core requirement.
2. YWE Core may validate WRW records as reference-profile evidence but cannot
   derive neutral engine truth from the profile alone.
3. Runtime change occurs in scoped worldstate, branch state, beings,
   resonance, access, perception, and consequence. It does not rewrite the
   fixed realm ontology.
4. Perception, myth, prophecy, and faction claims remain labeled
   interpretations until a governing worldstate contract records an objective
   change.
5. When a summary conflicts with either canonical source, the canonical source
   controls. When WRW prose conflicts with ASH or YWE Core authority, the
   upstream or Core authority controls within its own scope.

## Change Discipline

Canon changes require a deliberate revision to the applicable canonical source
with provenance and scope review. A summary, schema example, generated output,
host presentation, or runtime interpretation cannot silently amend these
boundaries.
